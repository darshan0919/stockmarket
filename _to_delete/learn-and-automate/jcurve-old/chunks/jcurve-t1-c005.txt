Supply Bottlenecks -Where is the actual constraint: land, power, permits, cooling, or substation capacity? -How much capacity is energized today vs. still a “paper pipeline”? -Are Mumbai/Chennai landing station adjacencies already spoken for, forcing spillover into Tier-2 hubs? 3. Value Capture -Who captures economics: telcos (Airtel Nxtra, Jio), infra JVs (AdaniConneX), or global players (NTT, Equinix)? -Is this an oligopoly with pricing power, or a capacity arms race that leads to compression? -Where’s the moat: land + power, interconnect richness, SLA reliability, or renewable PPAs? 4. Pricing & Contracts -What’s the real Rs/kW-month after incentives and free rent? -Are contracts 5–10 years take-or-pay with escalators, or shorter and repriced? -How sensitive are margins to a 15–20% price drop if supply overshoots? 5.
