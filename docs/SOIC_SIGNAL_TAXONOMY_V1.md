## What this means for building

- Biggest gap is G4/G5 (technical entries and exits) and G0 (regime): almost all NEW. They need only OHLCV + scan counts, so they are script-only and cost no LLM tokens once defined.
- G2 is largely done; remaining work is wiring (F3 decay flag, F6 percentile, F8 persistence).
- Exit evidence is thin in the corpus: one strong Suresh checklist (X1-X4) plus a distribution post (X5). P2 exit pass should confirm and extend.

## What could be wrong

- Thresholds (2 months base, 5-6% range, 60 days) come from single posts and are unvalidated; authors may disagree (Suresh is thematic/fundamental, thechartist is trend-following technical).
- Many signals are standard TA; calibrating them on a few months of data will mislead, so P6 needs longer history and a benchmark.
- G0 gates could block good entries in sharp reversals; test before enforcing M3.
