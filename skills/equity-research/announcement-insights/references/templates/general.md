CATEGORY: general (uncategorized)
No category-specific extraction checklist exists yet for this announcement's actual
subject matter — that does NOT lower the bar, it changes the method:

1. Read the PDF fully and identify the single most decision-relevant fact; quantify it.
2. Cross-reference this company's notes DB (`get-company-notes`) for continuity —
   does this confirm, extend, or contradict a prior note?
3. If the PDF references something outside the document itself (an industry dynamic,
   a peer's precedent, a regulation you don't recognise), use your knowledge base and,
   if genuinely uncertain, a web search against a credible source (exchange filings,
   the company's own investor-relations page, a recognised financial news outlet) —
   do NOT guess or fabricate context. Cite what you used in one clause.
4. If, after all that, the document is genuinely immaterial, mark significance
   `routine` and state in one line why it carries no signal.
5. If you find yourself writing this note for the 3rd+ time this month for a subject
   matter that clearly deserves its own checklist (e.g. a recurring theme like
   "litigation settlement" or "land monetisation"), say so explicitly in the insight's
   final sentence ("no dedicated template yet for <subject> — candidate for a new
   category") — `insight-validation`'s nightly review looks for exactly this signal
   when proposing new templates to add to this library.
