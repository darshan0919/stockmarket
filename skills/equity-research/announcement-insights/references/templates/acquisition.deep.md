CATEGORY: acquisition — DEEP [HIGH CONVICTION]
Read `references/demerger-merger-management-change-playbook.md` Part 2 before writing.
Produce:

1. **Deal mechanics** — target business description, stake % acquired, deal value,
   structure (cash / stock / earn-out / deferred), funding source (cash on hand / debt
   — check leverage impact / equity — check dilution).
2. **Valuation paid** — EV/revenue and/or EV/EBITDA implied by the deal value vs
   target's disclosed financials; compare against the ACQUIRER's own trading multiple
   (accretive if paid multiple < acquirer's multiple, dilutive if higher) and against
   sector precedent transactions if known.
3. **Strategic rationale** — is the stated rationale (market access, tech/IP,
   capacity, vertical integration) credible given the acquirer's existing business, or
   is it a diversification move outside its circle of competence?
4. **Related-party check** — any promoter/group ownership overlap with the target?
5. **Integration/consolidation timeline and risk** — expected close date, regulatory
   gates, integration risk flagged by management or inferable from the deal structure.
6. **Bear case + what to watch next** — one dated catalyst (close date, first
   post-acquisition quarter to show consolidated numbers).
   significance floor: medium. Tag `high_conviction`.
