CATEGORY: management_change — DEEP [HIGH CONVICTION]
Read `references/demerger-merger-management-change-playbook.md` Part 3 before writing.
Produce:

1. **Who/what changed** — role, incumbent, effective date, stated reason, successor
   (named or "not yet named" — flag the latter).
2. **Governance red-flag check** — explicitly score each: abrupt exit before term end?
   Auditor resignation (especially citing cooperation/information-access issues —
   treat as high-signal)? Pattern of ≥2 KMP exits within the last 2 quarters (pull
   from notes history)? Divergence between stated reason and any market chatter?
3. **If it's a credible new hire** — assess whether this looks like a
   professionalization/scaling move (external hire with a relevant scale-up track
   record) that could be a LEADING INDICATOR of a future demerger/reorg — cross-check
   notes history for any related corporate-simplification signals, and flag this as a
   forward-looking watch item even if there's no concrete follow-through yet.
4. **"First turn around, seldom turn" check** — if this is framed as a turnaround
   trigger, what concrete execution evidence (market share, margins, order book)
   exists YET, versus just the leadership change itself? Don't upgrade conviction
   on the appointment alone.
5. **Bear case + what to watch next** — one dated catalyst (next concall where the
   new leadership's plan should be articulated, or the successor's expected joining
   date).
significance floor: medium. Tag `high_conviction`.
