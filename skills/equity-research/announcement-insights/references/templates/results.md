CATEGORY: results
Extract: period; Revenue, EBITDA, EBITDA margin, PAT — both YoY and QoQ; EPS; key segment
drivers; and any outlook commentary. Flag beats/misses vs guidance captured in prior notes.

Inventory Gains check (mandatory): when EBITDA/PAT margin expansion is a headline driver of
the result, explicitly check whether it is inflated by inventory (stock) gains — revaluation
of existing raw-material/finished-goods inventory upward on rising commodity/input prices.
Signals: a jump in Other Income/exceptional items, margin expansion that outpaces stated
volume growth, or explicit filing/PR language on "inventory gains"/"stock gains". Quantify
the estimated PAT/EBITDA contribution where derivable, and state in the insight whether the
result is inventory-gain-driven (non-recurring) or structural/operating (sustainable) —
this distinction must be surfaced, not buried, since inventory gains reverse when commodity
prices fall.
