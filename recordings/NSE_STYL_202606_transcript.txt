2. Chairman's Strategic Review

Q1 FY27 Business Highlights:
- Revenue Growth: 21.1% Year-on-Year (YoY) increase
- Margin Pressures: Impact of global geopolitical tensions on gross margins, material cost escalations
- Facility Infrastructure: Construction progress at Nagpur and Bengaluru facilities
- Major Contract Wins: Two multi-year tenders from leading PSP banks, ~INR 73 crores over contract period

Metal Card and Premiumization Strategy: Payment Solutions segment = 42% of total revenue. Metal Cards expansion targeting affluent Gen Z and wealth management demographics.

IoT Segment: 145% YoY growth. Lalwani: "Within our IoT business, RFID continues to emerge as a significant long-term opportunity... We believe the market is still in the early stage of adoption and offers substantial headroom for growth."

3. Financial Performance

CFO Pawan Kumar:

- Gross Margin: 41.7% vs 44.5% in Q1 FY26
- EBITDA: 94 Crores (25.1% Margin)

Capital: 369 crores cash. IPO proceeds deployed: 24.4 crores (CAPEX: 6.7 cr, GCP: 13.7 cr, Issue Expenses: 3.9 cr).

4. Q&A Session

SIM Card & Telecom: SEASAI fulfilling 20-25% of SIM card requirements (pre-paid and post-paid) for India's third-largest telecom operator.

UPI vs Physical Cards: "UPI saturation level has almost hit the ceiling... we also feel strongly that UPI does not directly impact card issuances. We are more concerned about card issuances rather than the number of transactions... as time goes by, MDR (Merchant Discount Rate) being charged on UPI could be another factor which should in fact even probably completely obliterate the difference between a UPI and a card."

IoT Growth: Long-term 45% CAGR target. IoT to contribute 15-18% of total revenue by end of FY27.

Bengaluru Facility: Construction nearing ready state. Certification in Q4 FY27. Meaningful revenue contribution anticipated FY28.

5. Closing Remarks

Capex plan: 140-160 crores for full year. Medium-term revenue growth target: 8-12%.
